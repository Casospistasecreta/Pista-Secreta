# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public Pista Secreta sales landing page (`/`)
- Crawl behavior and public static routes shipped by the web artifact

## Out of scope
- API endpoints and internal analytics views
- Post-purchase upsell pages as organic-search landing pages; these should remain accessible to buyers but should not compete in search results
- Design sandbox / mockup artifact

## Target audience
- Portuguese-speaking consumers in Brazil seeking immersive digital mystery and investigation games for couples, friends, and families

## Primary keywords
- jogo de investigação online
- jogo de mistério digital
- experiência investigativa para casal e amigos
- caso investigativo digital

## Crawler assumptions
- Search, social-preview, and AI crawlers must receive useful metadata and primary landing-page content without executing client-side JavaScript.
